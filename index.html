About me

Hi there! I'm Jurre, I did my BSc and MSc at the Wageningen University. During my Bachelors I focused on Hydrology and Geology in a more physics and modeling based approach. I really loved working with streams, flow velocities and soil water dynamics. I, however, discovered that I was very interested in the more hard skills like, coding and remote sensing. Therefore, I opted for the MSc program Geo Information Science. During this time I learned a lot on the whole process, from data gathering to final product. I consciously decided to gather skills besides my study to have a more diverse skillset. One of my biggest and what I enjoyed most were my board year and activity organization experiences.
